# 💄 Beauty and Cosmetics Sales Analysis Dashboard

An end-to-end Power BI sales analytics project designed to track, analyze, and visualize retail performance, revenue growth, product category trends, and customer purchasing behavior in the beauty and cosmetics industry.

---

## 📊 Project Overview
This dashboard provides actionable insights into sales performance across various beauty product categories. It helps stakeholders identify top-performing products, regional sales trends, peak shopping periods, and profit margins to optimize inventory and marketing strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI:** Data modeling, DAX measures, interactive visualizations, and dashboard design.
* **Excel / CSV:** Data cleaning, preliminary validation, and preprocessing.
* **GitHub:** Version control and project documentation.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics & Insights
* **Total Revenue & Profit:** Monitored overall business financial health with dynamic filtering.
* **Top Product Categories:** Identified highest-selling cosmetic lines and skin-care ranges.
* **Customer Behavior:** Analyzed repeat customer rates and average order values.
